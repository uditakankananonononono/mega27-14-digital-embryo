# Data manifest - mega27-14 digital embryo

Uniform counting rule: one accession-level record (unique identifier-backed
embryo / model / structure) fetched and used = 1 dataset.

## liu2013 - Liu, Morrison & Gregor, PNAS 2013 ("Dynamic interpretation of
maternal inputs by the Drosophila segmentation gene network")
- Source: Zenodo record 4942019 (mirror of Dryad doi:10.5061/dryad.2j9p4).
  https://zenodo.org/api/records/4942019
- `LiveImaging.mat`: Bcd-GFP nuclear intensity gradients + cephalic-furrow
  positions, 29 fly lines, 628 embryos (two-photon live imaging, n.c. 14).
  -> 628 accession-level datasets (one per embryo gradient).
- `Immunofluorescence.mat`: Hb/Kr/Gt/Kni + Eve protein profiles, 1000-point
  AP axis, dorsal+ventral, 6 fly lines (embryo count TBD after parse).
- License: CC0 (Dryad/Zenodo deposit). Citation: Liu F, Morrison AH,
  Gregor T. PNAS 110(15):6024-6029 (2013).
