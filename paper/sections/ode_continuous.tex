\subsection{Continuous equations change the Boolean rule, and the feedback threshold depends on parameters}
The reduced five-node Boolean core and the Hill ODE share a signed-edge list, not a transition semantics. The Boolean successor turns a node on for positive signed input, off for negative input, and preserves its current value when the signed sum is zero. This tie rule gives persistence without an explicit self-edge. In particular, $(wg,en,hh,ptc,ci)=(1,1,1,0,0)$ is a Boolean fixed point despite ci=0. The ODE instead has linear decay and no production without activation: at that same state, without added feedback, $\dot{wg}=-1$. Removing zero-input persistence while introducing a continuous production function changes two assumptions at once. A differing outcome does not isolate discreteness or show that real segment-polarity bistability requires a particular biological feedback strength. This five-node reduction is also not the fourteen-node public network used for the exact basin census above.

The old records in \path{results/ode_vs_boolean.json} and \path{results/ode_vs_boolean_scan.json} are retained as historical finite-grid terminal calculations. A finite set of initial values ending near one rounded state does not prove a single global attractor. The current tracked base experiment specifies an Euler integration, clipping and approximate rounded final-state grouping; it does not certify stationary points by their residuals or prove their stability. The threshold-scan JSON is not accompanied by a tracked scan-generating script specifying its added autoactivation term and parameter cross-product. Its unqualified reading that every tested parameter pair requires $a\geq.9$ is therefore withdrawn, as is the biological near-full-strength requirement. No archived number is changed.

To make the comparison inspectable, a fresh local plan defines a separate continuous model explicitly. Let $H(x)=x^n/(K^n+x^n)$. Its five derivatives are
\begin{align*}
\dot{wg}&=\min(1,H(ci)+aH(wg))-wg,\\
\dot{en}&=H(wg)-en,\quad \dot{hh}=H(en)-hh,\\
\dot{ptc}&=H(ci)(1-H(hh))-ptc,\quad \dot{ci}=-ci.
\end{align*}
The last equation follows the production convention that a node with no positive activator has zero production. It shows directly that this continuous model cannot maintain positive ci: that is a modeling choice, not an observed biological loss of expression. An added wg term creates a different feedback system; it is not a calibrated strength of cis-regulation. Each term, saturation convention and decay rate must be stated before a threshold means anything.

The frozen plan scans four $K$ values, three Hill exponents, five added strengths ($a=0,.5,.85,.9,1$), and all $3^5=243$ starts on $\{0,.5,1\}^5$. We integrate to $t=100$ using unclipped fourth-order Runge--Kutta at step sizes .1 and .05. The 120 parameter/step-size records count terminal wg above .5 and record full-vector residuals separately. The largest endpoint difference after halving the step is $1.875\times10^{-8}$, small for this protocol. The largest absolute derivative at the horizon is $1.015\times10^{-4}$, so we do not label every terminal vector a certified equilibrium. Rounded endpoint counts remain descriptive rather than attractor counts.

Without added feedback ($a=0$) the terminal wg-ON fraction is zero at every sampled setting, consistent with the earlier qualitative negative but not a global-attraction proof. At $K=.2$, $a=.85$ already gives 88.889\% for each tested exponent. At $K=.65$ with $n=2$ or 4, even $a=1$ gives zero. At $K=.5,n=2$, the $a=1$ fraction is 66.667\%, but its largest derivative residual is the audit maximum, so a near-threshold endpoint should not be promoted to a proven stable attractor. The new term is explicitly stated and not asserted to reconstruct the undocumented old threshold scan. These results demonstrate model and parameter dependence; they neither fit actual cellular rates nor validate a developmental intervention.

All settings, endpoint discrepancies, residuals, coordinate ranges and rounded endpoint counts are in \path{results/grn_ode_semantics_audit.json}, generated by \path{experiments/grn_ode_semantics_audit.py}, with frozen-plan hash. Tests verify the Boolean persistence counterexample, derivatives at the same state, solver step-halving and representative saved outcomes. A more meaningful biological comparison would require a justified mapping between rules and kinetics, a measured phenotype and parameters, and stability/continuation analysis beyond this finite grid. That evidence is absent and remains an open gap.
