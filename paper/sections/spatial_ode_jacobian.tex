\subsection{Native Jacobian failure and local finite-difference spectra}
A frozen local linearization unit tests the unchanged time-11,000 endpoint, not a new parameter setting. The endpoint reproduces the previous continuation exactly and retains maximum absolute derivative $2.79\times10^{-9}$. libRoadRunner's native full Jacobian has the expected $132\times132$ geometry, but 1,320 entries are nonfinite: ten columns fail in all 132 rows. These columns correspond to EN in cells 1, 2 and 4, CN in cell 3, and PTC1 through PTC6 in cell 3. No native-Jacobian spectrum or mode count is inferred from this invalid matrix. Near-zero concentrations and fractional-power kinetics make derivative numerics a concern; this observation does not establish the precise cause of the native failure.

The prespecified independent finite-difference checks keep all 132 species. Relative step sizes $10^{-5}$ and $10^{-6}$ use a concentration scale floor of $10^{-8}$, with forward differences where a central step would cross zero. The two matrices are finite and differ by at most $2.05\times10^{-6}$ in an entry. Their spectral abscissae are both approximately $-0.010861$; all 132 computed modes have real part less than $-10^{-8}$, with no positive or near-zero-real modes at that threshold. Respectively 48 and 46 columns use forward differences. This is agreement between two local numerical approximations, not a comparison against a valid native derivative matrix.

The finite-difference spectra supply local evidence for decaying linear modes at this selected endpoint. They do not certify an exact equilibrium, differentiability on the boundary, nonlinear or global attraction, biological robustness, or a rescue of ptc's effective spatial tie. The native-Jacobian failure remains a separate negative result, rather than being replaced by an unconditional stability claim.
