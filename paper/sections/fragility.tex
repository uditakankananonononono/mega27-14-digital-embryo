\section{The developmental fragility index: quantifying, not lamenting, the small wild-type basin}
The wild-type wg-ON basin of the published id-021 segment-polarity network (condition hh1\_WG0\_SLP0) contains exactly 128 of 16{,}384 states (0.781\%), enumerated exhaustively under synchronous update. Rather than report this as a bare negative, we quantify its structure as a \emph{developmental fragility index}: the fraction of single-node perturbations of basin states whose trajectory exits the wild-type basin,
\begin{equation}
F = \frac{1}{|B|\,n}\sum_{s \in B}\sum_{i=1}^{n} \mathbb{1}\left[\mathrm{traj}(s \oplus e_i) \notin B\right],
\end{equation}
where $B$ is the wild-type basin and $s \oplus e_i$ flips node $i$. We find $F = 0.500$: exactly half of the 1{,}792 single-bit perturbations destroy the wild-type outcome. Fragility is concentrated, not uniform (Table~\ref{tab:fragility}): Seven nodes have exit fraction exactly one: CIA, CIR, CI protein, EN protein, ci, en and wg. The other seven have exit fraction zero under this synchronous basin measure. The earlier four-node table hid three equally sensitive nodes and is corrected below.

\subsection{Initial-state restrictions are not maintained knockouts}
The historical \texttt{knockout\_predictions} field has seven initial-state restrictions with zero wg-ON fixed-point outcomes and six with twice the unrestricted fraction. The implementation does not maintain the named node clamp during its trajectory: \texttt{wt\_basin} sets the initial value, while \texttt{attractor\_of} calls the original successor rule at each step. For example, with every free node initially zero except en=1, the very first successor sets en=0. A maintained en=1 intervention would restore en to one after every update. This explicit source-level counterexample is retained in \path{results/grn_intervention_semantics_audit.json}.

Accordingly the previous knockout, constitutive-expression and basin-doubling interpretation is withdrawn. The old numbers remain unchanged and describe conditioning on a node's starting value, with a reduced initial-state denominator, not modifying the regulatory dynamics. A maintained-clamp experiment requires an explicit altered transition function and a fresh exact enumeration. It cannot be inferred from these records.

The edge-replacement experiment separately substitutes a regulator literal with zero in the target expression. This is a specified rule edit, not a physically validated deletion of an interaction. Its archived basin changes remain local model calculations, not evidence that targeted repression removal makes actual development more robust. The fragility index itself remains an exact synchronous single-bit basin-exit measure for the stated condition, not a measured biological fragility rate.

\subsection{An exact reference-state check separates marker retention from state recovery}
The previous maintained-clamp census deliberately used wg=1 at a fixed point as its endpoint. A new frozen-plan audit asks which complete fixed point is reached instead. Under the unrestricted condition hh external=1, WG external=0, SLP=0, the original model has one wg-ON fixed point and exactly 128 of 16,384 starts reach it. We identify it by every one of the fourteen free coordinates, not merely by a fate label. Its ON coordinates are CIA, CI protein, PH, PTC protein, SMO, WG protein, ci, ptc and wg; CIR, EN protein, HH protein, en and hh are OFF. The named software reference is not an experimentally established wild-type phenotype.

For each of the 28 maintained interventions, all 8,192 starts already matching the forced value are classified by their exact terminal fixed-point integer or by a cycle label. We score both complete reference equality and equality only on the thirteen nonmanipulated coordinates. The latter deliberately permits a disagreement at the forced node, while exposing downstream mismatches that marker wg cannot detect. A clamped value opposite to the original reference makes full-state equality impossible by construction. It need not make projected equality impossible. These tests answer different questions and must not be mixed in an intervention ranking.

Identifying a biological phenotype, measuring intervention kinetics and testing transient rather than permanent forcing remain separate research steps.

\subsection{Transient forcing followed by release changes the intervention question}
Permanent forcing changes the transition graph indefinitely. It cannot establish that a short intervention returns a system to the original attractor once the forcing ends. A new plan therefore evaluates finite forcing and explicit release. We hold the original external condition and the exact fourteen-coordinate reference fixed, start from every one of the 16,384 unrestricted states with equal weight, overwrite the chosen coordinate at intervention onset, maintain it for a stated number of synchronous updates, then restore the original successor without any clamp. Each released state is classified by its eventual original attractor. Thus success is attraction after release, not wg occupancy while it is forced.

The grid uses all fourteen nodes, both binary values and durations one, two, four and eight, giving 112 finite interventions. Every condition shares the full 16,384-start denominator, so comparisons do not confuse a reduced-state conditional measure with a different population size. Overwriting the coordinate initially maps pairs of starts onto the same clamped state, but both original starts keep their original weight. This initial overwrite is part of the hypothetical intervention. The durations count synchronous updates, not measured minutes, protein lifetimes or developmental stages.

With no intervention, 128 starts, or .78125\%, reach the reference. CIA ON for one update gives 3.125\% after release, two updates 6.25\%, and four or eight updates 25\%. CI protein ON saturates at 12.5\% in the recorded four/eight-update conditions, while ci ON saturates at 6.25\%. EN protein OFF gives 3.125\% at every tested duration. These finite differences show dependence on intervention duration and node identity within this model; they do not identify a medically or developmentally useful dose. The complete grid retains OFF conditions with zero recovery rather than presenting only favorable interventions.

wg ON rises from 6.25\% after one forced update to 12.5\%, 50\% and 100\% after two, four and eight updates. The eight-update result is checked after the clamp has ended: all starts enter the original reference basin. This is stronger than the marker tautology of permanently forcing wg, but it remains a result of the stated single-cell binary rules and fixed external inputs. A finite-pulse biological claim would require intervention kinetics, coordinate meanings, measured starting-state distributions and phenotype validation. The code supplies none of those measurements.

PTC protein OFF illustrates another distinction. Under maintained forcing it could retain wg marker states that differed from the reference on other coordinates. Under finite forcing and release, the original dynamics recovers the reference from .78125\% of full starts at every recorded duration. This is a restoration of the original rule, not evidence that permanent knockouts are equivalent to temporary perturbations. Exact reference equality at the instant of release is recorded separately from eventual reference attraction: a state outside the reference can still lie in its basin. Counting only occupancy at release would conflate immediate expression matching with later model recovery.

The experiment retains every release-state count, the number of distinct released states, immediate reference occupancy and eventual reference counts in \path{results/grn_release_audit.json}. Its frozen plan and executable are \path{experiments/grn_release_plan.json} and \path{experiments/grn_release_audit.py}. Tests regenerate the exact graph-based grid and independently enumerate full scalar trajectories for wg ON for two updates and CIA ON for four updates. All prior maintained/initial-restriction/noisy result files remain intact. This adds a controlled temporal-intervention question, not a universal robustness law. Temporary forcing, permanent forcing, initial-value restriction and continuing random noise are four different processes and must not share an unqualified ``basin rescue'' number.

\subsection{Initial-measure sensitivity is separate from endpoint identity}
The uniform 16,384-state census is an exact counting measure, not a measured distribution of embryonic starting states. A further frozen audit keeps the original model, synchronous update and eventual exact-reference endpoint fixed while changing only the initial-state weights. We test independent bits with ON probabilities .25 and .75, and a uniform distribution on the 106 states within Hamming distance two of the original reference. The latter is deliberately reference-centered and cannot be treated as an unbiased embryo sample. Ten predeclared forcing conditions plus the unforced baseline are evaluated under all four measures. No measure is selected after its result.

\subsection{Deterministic sequential update orders alter the basin}
Synchronous timing is another assumption, separate from external context and initial-state weights. A frozen audit uses the original 100 external context and all 16,384 internal starts but updates the fourteen nodes in place through a complete deterministic sweep. Three orders are retained: source order, reversed source order and a fixed permutation from seed 2183. A forced node is overwritten initially and after every microstep, then released after the stated number of complete sweeps. The original reference is still a fixed point in every order. These are not random asynchronous trajectories, nor do sweeps have a physical duration equivalent to synchronous steps.

Stochastic asynchronous updates, schedules changing between sweeps, kinetic rate models and independently measured developmental trajectories remain open. No timing convention in this audit validates a physical pulse or embryo rescue.

\subsection{Rule perturbations must pass endpoint-validity checks first}
A frozen preliminary audit changes one syntactically named regulator in one target rule to constant zero or one, replacing all occurrences of that regulator within that rule. The 29 distinct regulator--target references yield 58 counterfactual models, including external-input references. The original 100 external context and original internal reference are fixed. This is a Boolean-expression counterfactual, not an experimentally measured edge deletion or gene knockout; constants zero and one represent different changed logical assumptions.
